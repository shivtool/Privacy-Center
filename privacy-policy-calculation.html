This Privacy Policy explains how Shiv Computer ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the Calculation mobile application (the "App"), available on the Indus App Store and other platforms. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the terms described below.

1. Overview

Calculation is an offline utility app offering a suite of calculators — including basic, scientific, percentage, discount, profit/loss, GST, EMI, loan, SIP, simple and compound interest, currency conversion, unit conversion (length, weight, area, temperature), date difference, and BMI calculators. The App is designed to perform all calculations locally on your device.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require an account, login, or internet connection to function. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number

  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Any government ID, financial account, or payment information

If the App is distributed through an app store (such as the Indus App Store or Google Play), the store itself may collect limited technical data for installation, crash reporting, and analytics purposes (e.g. device model, OS version, app version, crash logs). This data is governed by that platform's own privacy policy, not directly by us. We do not operate our own analytics or advertising SDKs inside the App at this time.

4. Permissions Used by the App

The App requests only the minimum device permissions necessary for its core functionality (for example, storage access if you choose to export or save a report/receipt generated within the App). No permission is used to collect personal data for any purpose beyond that feature.

5. Data Storage

Any values you enter or history saved within the App (such as a calculation log) are stored locally on your device only. This data is not uploaded to our servers and is removed if you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party advertising networks or data-broker SDKs at this time. Should this change in a future update, this Privacy Policy will be revised in advance, and the update will be reflected on this page with a new "Last Updated" date.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose utility tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Data Security

Since the App does not transmit personal data to external servers, your information stays on your own device, protected by your device's own security features (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device software up to date.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You may uninstall the App at any time to remove all locally stored data associated with it. Because we do not hold any personal data on our servers, there is no remote account or data profile to request, export, or delete.
